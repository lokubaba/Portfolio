How diagnosing field sales pitch chaos led to a consolidated 1-URL web platform that replaced 10 open tabs, scaled across 13+ towers, and expanded to 100+ digital galleries across 25 tier-1 developers.

Sales reps juggled 10+ open tabs (PDFs, 3D flythroughs, pricing sheets). High-net-worth buyers experienced cognitive overload and hesitated on high-value commitments due to inability to visualize exact floor balcony view lines and daylight angles.

2. WHAT

A high-performance 1-URL web platform with 4 structured pillars: Neighbourhood (geospatial infra), Master Plan & Inventory (floor-level view lines), Amenities (360° virtual tours), and Collateral (instant custom PDF quote generator).

3. HOW

Shadowed live showroom pitches. Applied RICE scoring to strip non-essential 3D customization, cutting 6-month roadmap to a 4-month MVP (60 days early). Partnered with engineering on texture streaming and WebGL caching for sub-2s loads.

4. WHO

Primary Users: Enterprise sales managers, closing agents, and luxury homebuyers. Enterprise Clients: Hiranandani, Brigade, Century, Vasavi, Ankura, and Supreme to accelerate booking velocity.

Capstone: Porter First-Trip Conversion

An Airtribe PM Launchpad case study on lifting first-trip conversion for Porter’s individual moving customers from 41% to 58%, uncovering that trust—not pricing or fleet size—breaks conversion.

Five Discovery Methods, One Funnel View

Synthesized qualitative and quantitative insights from a 20-person survey, 8 depth interviews, an end-to-end app/web journey teardown, and competitive analysis against 7 moving logistics competitors.

The Root Discovery

Trust, not pricing, breaks conversion. Users reported severe pre-move anxiety: unconfirmed truck arrival dates, opaque additional charges upon arrival, and complete blackout between booking confirmation and driver assignment.

Persona: The Anxious First-Timer

First-time house mover relocating within Bengaluru/Pune. High stakes, sensitive personal belongings. Needs transparent price lock guarantees and real-time reassurance before parting with deposit money.

Persona: The Pragmatic Comparer

Comparing Porter against local packers and Agarwal Packers. Drops off when quoted an arbitrary lump sum without transparent breakdown of labor, toll, fuel, and handling charges.

RICE scored 8 distinct drop-off themes; narrowed down to the top two root barriers shown in indigo.

1. Untrustworthy Quote Step (RICE: 0.266)

The app hid final cost breakdowns behind multi-tap menus, and the web booking flow mandated a 24–48 hour tele-calling delay, shedding high-intent customers to instant competitors.

2. Blackout After Payment (RICE: 0.163)

Total information vacuum post-payment. Zero visibility into assigned driver identity, truck capacity, or milestone checklist during peak anxiety hours.
