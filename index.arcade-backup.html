Enterprise PropTech Platform

From Static Property Visuals to a Unified Digital Sales Experience

How I transformed a fragmented 10-tab presentation process into a single-URL enterprise sales journey, scoped a 6-month estimate into a 4-month MVP, and scaled across 13+ towers.

Shipped & Scaled to 4 Enterprise Projects
Client: House of Hiranandani • Platform: Relata

The Problem: Sales Teams Selling Blindly Across 10 Tabs

House of Hiranandani initially approached us for a visualization assignment. But through stakeholder discovery with their sales executives and directors, I uncovered the deeper product problem: sales reps relied on disconnected images, brochures, and walkthrough videos. When a buyer asked about a balcony view, tower orientation, or nearby schools, the rep was forced to shuffle between separate tabs and PDFs. The entire conversion hinged on asking the buyer to imagine what they couldn't see.

PILLAR 01
Neighbourhood
Contextual 3D GIS mapping showing schools, hospitals, transit hubs, and travel times without leaving the sales screen.
PILLAR 02
Inventory
Real-time tower navigation, floor plates, unit-level balcony orientations, and daylight simulations for exact buyer reassurance.
PILLAR 03
Amenities
Interactive clubhouse and landscape walkthroughs mapped to physical layout zones rather than generic static photo galleries.
PILLAR 04
Collateral
Instant dynamic brochure generation, unit pricing sheets, and payment schedules sent to the prospect’s WhatsApp/email in one click.

MVP Scoping & Engineering Alignment

  • Pruned a 6-month estimate down to 4 months: Engineering initially scoped the complete 4-pillar experience at 6 months. By facilitating RICE prioritization on essential high-converting flows (Neighbourhood + Inventory unit viewer), we launched an operational MVP in 4 months.
  • Phased Refinement Model: Dedicated the subsequent 2 months to secondary amenities and automated PDF exports based on live sales floor telemetry.
  • Single-URL Architecture: Replaced multiple native apps and heavy desktop installs with a web-first experience: 1 laptop, 1 URL, zero setup latency.

Adoption & Commercial Scale

  • Surge in Sales Adoption: Onsite reps immediately adopted the tool as their primary pitch deck, eliminating manual collateral preparation.
  • Expanded to 4 Flagship Developments: Due to measurable sales acceleration, House of Hiranandani awarded Relata four additional major projects, including massive residential townships spanning 13+ towers.
  • Client Retention: Established Relata as the long-term enterprise technology partner rather than a one-time visual asset vendor.
PM Retrospective: What I Would Do Differently Today

"If executing this project today, I would keep the discovery and problem-framing rigor unchanged, but incorporate AI-assisted rapid prototyping (dynamic UI mockups and automated data schemas) to compress the initial discovery-to-MVP cycle even further. Understanding the customer's sales friction and rallying cross-functional teams around a phased MVP remains the defining job of the PM."

The Trust Cliff: Why First-Time Movers Drop Off

Porter's on-demand experience thrives for repeat business users with built-up intuition. But first-time individual customers make high-stakes judgment calls under extreme stress on moving day. Through a 5-method research synthesis (12 surveys, 8 deep interviews, 9-stage funnel teardown, and 7-competitor benchmark), I proved that drop-offs were driven not by logistics capacity, but by a severe trust gap.

Solution 01: Pricing Trust RICE 0.425 (#1 Ranked)

Transparent Itemized Price Breakdown + Guaranteed Price-Lock Badge

Eliminates the fear of hidden on-the-day upcharges by exposing the underlying pricing logic directly inline before payment.

Today Single opaque price hidden behind tap-through; web requires 24–48h callback.
Shipped v1 Inline itemized breakdown (base + labor + tolls) + visible Price-Lock badge carry-through.
Solution 02: Pre-Move Anxiety RICE 0.298 (#2 Ranked)

Pre-Move Partner, Driver & Vehicle Reveal + 3-Stage Milestone Status

Removes the post-payment blackout where user anxiety peaks by immediately revealing driver credentials and moving progress.

Today Total blackout after payment: zero driver identity, no vehicle ETA or packing status.
Shipped v1 Driver card revealed upon partner assignment + 3-stage status (Packed → Loaded → In-Transit).

How I Operate

Product Management Philosophy

Principles refined across 10 years of bootstrapped P&L ownership, enterprise client delivery, and rigorous product discovery.

Discovery Precedes Solutioning

Never accept feature requests at face value. When clients asked for "more 3D renders," real user interviews revealed the actual pain was tab-switching friction on sales calls.

Ruthless MVP Definition

An MVP is the smallest slice of software that proves or disproves user value. Trimming Relata's 6-month roadmap to 4 months shipped value 60 days ahead of schedule.

Quantitative Prioritization (RICE)

Opinions are cheap; weighted scoring separates root-cause barriers from nice-to-haves. Prioritizing low-effort, high-impact data exposures drove the Porter business case.

Guardrails With North Stars

Optimizing for conversion without tracking cancellation and CSAT creates false wins. A healthy PM dashboard couples aggressive targets with strict operational guardrails.

Engineering Empathy

Trained as an engineer at IIT Guwahati, I speak the language of APIs, data schemas, and edge cases. Product managers earn respect by writing unambiguous acceptance criteria.

AI as Velocity Lever

AI is not a buzzword—it is an operational multiplier. At Zentra, integrating AI-assisted production pipelines reduced project turnaround by 30% while maintaining high delivery quality.
