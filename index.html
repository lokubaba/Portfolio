Capstone: Porter

An Airtribe PM Launchpad case study on lifting first-trip conversion for Porter’s first-time individual customers, from 41% to 58%. It is coursework built from my own research, not a Porter engagement.

Five methods, one funnel view

A 12-person survey, 8 follow-up interviews, a full app and web flow teardown, and a benchmark of 7 competitors across 8 feature areas.

The finding

Trust, not the physical service, breaks first-trip conversion. Three of 12 respondents independently said the vehicle arrived on a different day than promised, and damage, delays, support, and hidden charges recur across every competitor.

The anxious first-time renter

Has never booked a mover. Needs clear pricing, proof the provider will show up, and visibility after paying.

The budget-conscious shopper

Compares 3 or 4 options and needs an itemised price they can compare, not one opaque total.

RICE narrowed eight drop-off themes to two primary barriers, shown in blue.

1. An untrustworthy quote step

The app hides the breakdown behind a tap-through, and the web gives no price without a 24 to 48 hour callback.

2. Blackout after payment

No driver, vehicle, or status exactly when “will they show up” anxiety peaks.

Two additive screens, not a redesign. Pick a stage of the journey to see what changes.

With the change

Solution 1, itemised price with a Price-Lock badge, scored RICE 0.425. Solution 2, driver and vehicle reveal with milestone status, scored 0.298. Both use data Porter already holds.
